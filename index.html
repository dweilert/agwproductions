Grey's on 51st St.

When 19-year-old Noah Soloman stumbles upon the shocking truth that aliens are secretly living in his city, life takes a turn for the otherworldly. But things go from strange to deadly when one of them turns up murdered.

Noah is pulled into a high-stakes investigation that is anything but ordinary. With danger lurking in both human and extraterrestrial shadows, he must solve the mystery before the killer disappears.
