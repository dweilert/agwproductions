Alien Ghost Wizard Productions

Imaginative stories for adventurous readers.

Alien Ghost Wizard is a small publishing company for comics, graphic novels, and genre stories with a pulse of mystery, humor, cosmic weirdness, and heart.

The catalog begins with Grey's on 51st St., a sci-fi graphic novel mystery by Harris Baumann, and is built to grow into multiple books, series, formats, and reader campaigns.

Noah is pulled into a high-stakes investigation that is anything but ordinary. With danger lurking in both human and extraterrestrial shadows, he must solve the mystery before the killer disappears.
